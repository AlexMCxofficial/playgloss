How to play

PlayGloss is a game for two teams, Red and Blue, sharing one screen. Each turn a team spins the wheel and tries to pick the correct meaning of an English word. The first team to reach 100 points wins.

A turn

  1. The team whose turn it is spins the wheel. Red always goes first.
  2. The wheel lands on a difficulty level or a power-up.
  3. If it lands on a power-up, you keep it and the turn is over.
  4. If it lands on a level, you see the level first. If you own a 2x, 3x or Shield, you may use one now, before the word appears.
  5. The word appears with four possible meanings and a countdown. Pick one before time runs out.

Harder levels use rarer words. Points are only earned for a correct answer. A wrong answer or running out of time earns nothing, unless you used a Shield.

Power-ups

Power-upWhat it doesChance
2xDoubles the points if you answer correctly1.02%
3xTriples the points if you answer correctly0.68%
ShieldGives you half the points if you answer wrong. Halves can happen, such as 0.5 or 2.52.56%
Free 5Gives you 5 points straight away0.34%
  • You use 2x, 3x and Shield after you see the level and before you see the word.
  • Only one power-up can be active on a question.
  • A power-up is used up whether you answer right, wrong or run out of time. A 2x you did not get right is lost.
  • Unused power-ups stay with your team for later turns.

The wheel is drawn with equal slices, but the odds above are what really decide the result. Percentages are rounded, and a tiny remainder is left for something very rare.

Winning

  • When a team reaches 100 or more, the round is finished first, so Blue always gets the last turn.
  • After that round, the higher score wins.
  • If the scores are tied, each team spins once more in sudden death. The higher result wins, where a level counts as its points, Free 5 counts as 5 and other power-ups count as 0. A tie spins again.

Resign and draw

As in chess, on your turn, before you spin, you can resign (the other team wins) or offer a draw. If the other team accepts, the game ends as a draw. You can offer one draw per turn.

Download and install

You can always play in your browser on the Play page. Apps for offline play are published on the Releases page as they become available. They contain the whole game and need no internet connection.

Windows (.exe)

  1. Download the .exe from the Releases page and open it.
  2. If Windows shows a "protected your PC" warning because the app is not signed, choose More info, then Run anyway.

macOS (.dmg)

  1. Download the .dmg and open it.
  2. Drag PlayGloss to the Applications folder.
  3. The first time, macOS may say it cannot verify the app because it is not signed by Apple. Open System Settings, then Privacy & Security, scroll down and choose Open Anyway next to PlayGloss.
  4. If macOS says the app is damaged, run xattr -cr /Applications/PlayGloss.app in Terminal, then open it again.

Linux (.deb)

  1. Download the .deb.
  2. In a terminal, in the folder where it was saved, run sudo apt install ./FILENAME.deb with the real file name.

Android (.apk)

  1. Download the .apk on your phone and open it.
  2. If asked, allow your browser or file manager to install apps from this source.
  3. Tap Install.

iOS

iPhone and iPad installs depend on Apple's signing rules. Check the Releases page for the current option. Until then, play in the browser.

Privacy

PlayGloss collects nothing. There are no accounts, analytics, cookies, ads or trackers, and the game makes no network requests while you play. Your dark or light choice is saved only on your own device. Scores and answers exist only while the game is open. Read the full privacy notice.
