Settings

General

Theme
Color

Pick the accent color used for buttons and highlights. Text on top of it turns black or white automatically so it stays readable.

OLED uses pure black and Extreme uses dark red with a red accent. Choosing either one switches to Dark, and choosing Light leaves it.

These choices are saved only on this device and are never sent anywhere.

Modes

Modes change the rules of the game. They are off by default, so you have to turn them on yourself.

Mode
Infinite

Extreme flips how often the levels come up: Impossible comes as often as Very Easy does in Default, and the other levels flip the same way. Points, time per level and the Easter egg odds stay the same. Choosing Extreme also switches the color to Extreme. Choosing Default puts back the look you had before, unless you changed it yourself. You can still use the Extreme color in Default mode and the other way round.

Infinite works with Default and Extreme. There is no 100 points goal: everyone has 3 hearts, and each wrong answer or time up costs one. The game goes on until the hearts are gone. The heart picture is from the Minecraft Wiki (see Credits).

When you host an online game, you pick its mode in the Host a game tab instead.

Play Online (opt-in)

Play with other people on the same Wi-Fi or network. One person hosts from the PlayGloss app, and everyone else joins from the Online option on the Play page (in the app or on the website), by scanning the QR code with any camera app, or by opening the link in any browser. Nothing goes through the internet and nothing is collected.

Same network

Hosting needs the PlayGloss app for Windows, macOS, Linux or Android, so this switch stays off in the browser. Joining does not need this switch: use Online, then Join a game, on the Play page.

Turning this on does not open anything by itself. A game can only be joined after you choose Online, then Host a game, on the Play page, you approve every person who asks to join, and it closes when you stop hosting, leave the page or close the app.

It is off by default. Only use it on a network you trust, such as your home Wi-Fi, and not on public Wi-Fi. Windows and macOS may ask you to allow network access the first time you host.
