Settings

General

Theme
Color

Pick the accent color used for buttons and highlights. Text on top of it turns black or white automatically so it stays readable.

OLED uses pure black and Extreme uses dark red with a red accent. Choosing either one switches to Dark, and choosing Light leaves it.

These choices are saved only on this device and are never sent anywhere.

Startup splash screen

Available in the installed PlayGloss app.

Fonts

Font
Size

The font and the size apply to the whole app. System is the default (your device's own font); 100% is the normal size and you can go from 75% to 150%. Helvetica, Arial, Segoe UI and Compasse are shown only if they are installed on your device (they are not shipped with PlayGloss, and Compasse is a commercial font), otherwise a similar font is used. Fira Code and JetBrains Mono are open source (SIL Open Font License), are shipped with PlayGloss and are monospace fonts.

Background

Type

Pick a solid background color from the presets, a hex code, the RGB sliders or the color wheel. Text and cards change to black or white automatically so they stay readable.

Choose a picture from your device. PlayGloss shrinks it to fit a screen and keeps it only on this device; it is never uploaded.

Fit
Blur
0

Scheme uses the background of your color scheme (the default). Color paints the page with one solid color. Image shows your own picture behind the app: Cover fills the whole screen and crops the edges, Contain shows the whole picture and fills the rest with the scheme color. Blur softens the background, from 0 (off, the default) to 10 (strongest); it is available for the Image type.

Documentation

Navigation

Choose which side the Documentation navigation sits on. On large screens it is a sidebar that you can switch on or off with the sidebar icon on the Documentation page; on tablets it is a drawer that opens with the Contents button.

On phones the navigation is always on the left, so this choice is greyed out.

Modes

Modes change the rules of the game. They are off by default, so you have to turn them on yourself.

Mode
Infinite

Extreme flips how often the levels come up: Impossible comes as often as Very Easy does in Default, and the other levels flip the same way. Points, time per level and the Easter egg odds stay the same. Choosing Extreme also switches the color to Extreme. Choosing Default puts back the look you had before, unless you changed it yourself. You can still use the Extreme color in Default mode and the other way round.

Infinite works with Default and Extreme. There is no 100 points goal: everyone has 3 hearts, and each wrong answer or time up costs one. The game goes on until the hearts are gone. The heart picture is from the Minecraft Wiki (see Credits).

When you host an online game, you pick its mode in the Host a game tab instead.

Play Online (opt-in)

Play with other people on the same Wi-Fi or network. One person hosts from the PlayGloss app, and everyone else joins from the Online option on the Play page (in the app or on the website), by scanning the QR code with any camera app, or by opening the link in any browser. Nothing goes through the internet and nothing is collected.

Same network

Hosting needs the PlayGloss app for Windows, macOS, Linux or Android, so this switch stays off in the browser. Joining does not need this switch: use Online, then Join a game, on the Play page.

Turning this on does not open anything by itself. A game can only be joined after you choose Online, then Host a game, on the Play page, guest approval is required by default, and the room closes when you stop hosting, leave the page or close the app.

It is off by default. Only use it on a network you trust, such as your home Wi-Fi, and not on public Wi-Fi. Windows and macOS may ask you to allow network access the first time you host.

Advanced Mode

Advanced Mode unlocks deep customization for Custom games. It is off by default and changes nothing until you turn it on and switch on Custom rules on the Play page.

Advanced Mode

Advanced Mode is on. With Custom rules switched on, an Advanced tab appears before Teams. In it you can:

  • change the chance of every level and of 2x, 3x, Shield and Free 5 (in percent, up to two decimals, and together exactly 100%; you always see how much is left to give out),
  • switch the surprise slice "?" on or off,
  • play the built-in words (Default) or your own multiple-choice questions with 2, 3 or 4 choices (Custom), and import or export them as a word list,
  • export or import all of it as Advanced Custom Mode Configurations.

Everything stays on this device. Turning Advanced Mode off hides the tab and the games go back to the standard odds and words (your choices are kept for next time).
