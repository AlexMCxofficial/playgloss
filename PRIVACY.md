# Privacy

PlayGloss collects nothing.

- No analytics, cookies, ads or trackers.
- No accounts. The game makes no network requests while you play.
- The only thing stored is your dark/light choice, kept on your own device and never sent anywhere.

Where you get the game from may keep its own standard server logs (for example your IP address). That is outside the game's code:

- GitHub: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement
- Our website host: see its privacy policy.
